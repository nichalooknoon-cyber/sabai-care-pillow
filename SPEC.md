# Specification — SabaiCare Pillow

## Constitution
Accessibility first; bilingual parity; no medical diagnosis/treatment claims; privacy by default; simple static deployment.

## Requirements
- Audience: older adults and their families.
- Languages: Thai and English.
- Business hours: 08:00–20:00 daily.
- Style: warm/natural + senior friendly; large readable text and large controls.
- Finder: visual body-area cue plus large symptom/need buttons.
- Needs covered: neck, shoulder, back, lower back, knee, snoring support, reflux comfort, side-sleep discomfort, leg support, seated back support.
- Recommendation output: pillow type, visual, benefit, and reason for recommendation.
- Catalog: 8 pillow types with sample pricing.
- Sections: Home, Find Your Pillow, Products, About, Health Tips, Contact.
- Contact placeholders: phone, LINE, Facebook; prominent advice CTA.
- Safety copy: general comfort/support information only; not medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Implementation plan
Static HTML/CSS/JS for reliable GitHub Pages hosting. Recommendation mapping executes locally in the browser and sends no health selections to a server.
